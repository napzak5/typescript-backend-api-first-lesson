A sale can be paid in parts (some in cash, the rest by card); the system knows what is left to pay, refuses more than that, and shows whether nothing, a part or all of the sale was received. Before a sale is entered, the till can ask what it would come to: the services of the booking at the prices they were booked at, any services added at the prices of the menu, the consumption tax of the salon added once to the sum (fractions of a yen dropped), and which kinds of payment method take that total in one payment. The points of a customer are one balance for the whole platform, as a customer belongs to no salon: the staff of any salon look up a customer's balance and history, earn points for them and use their points at the till, naming the sale or the booking the points are for, and the owner, a manager or an administrator adjusts a balance by hand, with a reason. Points are not earned on or used for a void sale or a cancelled booking, a sale or a booking is earned on once and used for once, and a balance never goes below 0. Entering a sale or taking a payment by points does not move the balance; the till records the points as a movement of its own. 1. **Understand Context First**: Ask clarifying questions about salon type (beauty/hair/nail/spa), size (independent/chain), target market (luxury/mass market), and geographical considerations
